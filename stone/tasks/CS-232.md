## Description

One shared rule for the size of an answer (ADR-21): a new engine module `tiers` prints critical parts whole, shrinks only optional parts, and ends a cut answer with one line that names what it left out and how large it is. `--budget` (tokens, chars / 4) stays as the override. First users: the flat read commands through `fit_lines` (CS-229) and `code explain`, whose budget does not hold today. `stone brief` (CS-202) builds on it.

The rule, in characters so that it is exact (`limit = budget * 4`; a line costs its chars plus 1 for the newline; a heading costs the same way and is printed only when at least one line of its part is printed; a part with no lines prints nothing and is never named):

- R1. No budget, or the cost of all parts is at most `limit`: print every part whole, in the given order, with no notice.
- R2. Otherwise critical parts are printed whole, always.
- R3. Notice forms. Long: `left out: <names, joined by ", "> (<n> lines, about <t> tokens); raise --budget`. Short: `left out: <n> lines, about <t> tokens; raise --budget`. `n` is the count of dropped lines, `t` is the sum over cut parts of `tokens(dropped lines joined by "\n")`, and `1 lines` is written `1 line`. The worst notice is the notice with every optional part dropped whole. Use the long form when `critical cost + cost(worst long notice) <= limit`; otherwise the short form.
- R4. `room = limit - critical cost - cost(worst notice in the chosen form)`, not below 0.
- R5. Take the optional parts by full cost, smallest first (equal cost: the earlier part first). For each: `share = room / parts not yet handled`; keep the largest prefix of its lines whose cost, heading included, is at most `share`; subtract the cost of what was kept from `room`.
- R6. Print the parts in the given order with their kept lines, then the notice as the last line when at least one line was dropped.
- Guarantee: `tokens(text) <= max(budget, tokens(critical parts + notice))`.

Worked example (the expected values of step 3). Parts: `task` critical, lines `["task CS-1"]` (cost 10); `doc text` optional, ten lines of 19 chars (cost 200); `precedents` optional, two lines of 9 chars (cost 20). Budget 40: limit 160, total 230, so cut. Worst long notice `left out: doc text, precedents (12 lines, about 55 tokens); raise --budget` is 74 chars, cost 75; 10 + 75 <= 160, so the long form. Room 75. `precedents` first: share 37, both lines kept (20), room 55. `doc text`: share 55, two lines kept (40). Text: `task CS-1`, two doc lines, two precedent lines, then `left out: doc text (8 lines, about 40 tokens); raise --budget` (8 lines of 19 chars joined by 7 newlines are 159 chars, 40 tokens). The whole text is 132 chars, 33 tokens.
