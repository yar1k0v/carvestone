## Description

An agent gets the context of its task in one or two calls, and trusts the answer. Done when the three stages of ADR-21 are in place and measured.

1. Trust: wrong and empty answers are fixed (CS-223, CS-203, CS-204, CS-185), a level-1 `edit --section` no longer drops nested sections without a warning, `--budget` works on every read command, and an answer names what Stone does not index.
2. Map: Stone prints a project map for the start of a session.
3. Brief: `stone brief` (CS-202) returns the task brief and the task pack, with tiers and the ready check.

Before stage 1: take the baseline of turns and tokens per task from the transcripts.
